## Vision

_To fill in once the architecture and stack are chosen._ Cover which features we'll build and to what level (Simplex, Medium or Ultimo), what providers get from it, the frontend and backend architecture, how we'll deploy it, and which qualities (maintainability, scalability, reusability) the design aims for.

## Success metrics

_Draft:_
- Every feature in scope passes its "Required evidence" check in the SRS during the demo.
- Fewer than 10% of Done tasks are invalid (skipped stages, unticked Definition of Done, or missing assignee, due date or estimate).
- At least two stand-up records per week, uploaded within 24 hours.
- API contract published on time for both milestones.
- Every member has commits and reviewed tasks in data model, architecture, UX/UI, report and development.

## Risks

_Draft:_
- Delays from the Customer Portal team on shared APIs. Agree the contract early and mock their side until it's ready.
- Uneven contribution. Track it on the board each week.
- Exams and holidays over Christmas and New Year, which fall inside Milestone 2.
- Multi-machine deployment issues found late. Dockerize from the first sprint of Milestone 2.

## Team rules

_Draft:_
- Stand-ups twice a week (e.g. Monday and Thursday), uploaded to the board within 24 hours.
- Day-to-day sync in the team channel (_name_).
- Every task in Sprint Backlog or later has an assignee, a due date, an estimate, a reviewer and a Definition of Done. Update the status as soon as it changes.
- Reply to team messages within 24 hours on working days.
- Missed deadline: first, a smaller task due within 48 hours. If that is missed too, we report to the course coordinator.
